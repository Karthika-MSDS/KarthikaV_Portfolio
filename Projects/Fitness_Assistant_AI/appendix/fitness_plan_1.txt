**Workout Plan (Beginner, Female, No Equipment, 5 Days)**

**Day 1 – Full Body**
- Warm-up: March in Place – 3 min
- Glute Bridge – 3x10
- Push-ups – 3x8
- Squats – 3x12
- Mountain Climbers – 3x15
- Cool-down: Forward Fold – 3 min

**Day 2 – Lower Body**
- Warm-up: High Knees – 3 min
- Lunges – 3x10
- Step-Back Lunge – 3x10
- Wall Sit – 3x15s
- Calf Raises – 3x12
- Cool-down: Butterfly Stretch – 3 min

**Day 3 – Core**
- Warm-up: Jumping Jacks – 3 min
- Plank – 3x15s
- Bicycle Crunches – 3x10
- Bird Dog – 3x10
- Side Plank – 3x10s/side
- Cool-down: Cat-Cow – 3 min

**Day 4 – Upper Body**
- Warm-up: Arm Circles – 3 min
- Push-ups – 3x8
- Superman – 3x10
- Tricep Dips (chair) – 3x10
- Glute Bridge – 3x12
- Cool-down: Child’s Pose – 3 min

**Day 5 – Cardio & Mobility**
- Warm-up: March in Place – 3 min
- High Knees – 3x15
- Squats – 3x12
- Mountain Climbers – 3x15
- Lunges – 3x10
- Cool-down: Forward Fold – 3 min
